ABOUT US

DUA SABA IMPEX PLASTIC MACHINERY, Your Professional Blow Molding Machine & Preform Mold Service Provider in Pakistan

DUA SABA IMPEX PLASTIC MACHINERY is founded in 2012, located in Karachi city, Pakistan. We are professional Provider of PET bottle blowing machines, plastic molds, etc... Our machine are suitable for 5ml-25L plastic bottles, water bottles, carbonated beverage bottles, oil bottles, cosmetics bottles. We can equip machine from 1-12 cavity, output from hundreds bottles to 10000 bottles/hour, also provide full set of production system from preform production, bottle blowing, filling to final packaging. Our products has covered all over Pakistan and exported to more than 100 cities, including Karachi, Islamabad, Lahore, Quetta, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Gujranwala etc. We'd like to provide high quality solution to clients with our enthusiasm and rich experience. We sincerely hope to establish business relationship with friends all over the world.
