Injection Moulding Machines

Dua Saba Impex sources injection moulding machines across a wide clamping-force range — from compact 35-tonne units for small parts up to heavy 2800-tonne machines for large industrial mouldings. Our range covers general-purpose European-standard machines, servo energy-saving models, high-speed thin-wall machines, PET preform machines, fruit-crate machines, PVC pipe-fitting machines, and large two-platen machines.

Every machine we supply is sourced from experienced manufacturing partners, checked against the buyer's specification, and can be inspected before dispatch. Talk to us about your clamping-force, shot-volume, and mould-size requirements and we'll match you with the right series.
